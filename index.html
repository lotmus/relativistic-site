The Relativistic Investigation Bureau

Companion Demonstrations

Three interactive physics demos, built to go with The Murder That Hadn't Happened Yet (Book 1). Each one dramatizes a real concept from the book's Appendix — no sign-in, no account, just drag something until it misbehaves.

Lesson 2 · Special Relativity

Simultaneity & Spacetime

Why Derek's photograph and Weinstein's clock can't agree on "at the same time," and why neither of them is lying. Drag toward c and watch consensus reality quietly resign.

 Lesson 5 · General Relativity

Curvature & Geodesics

Gravity as geometry, not a force — which is a relief for the particle, since nothing is pulling it anywhere, it's just very committed to going straight through a universe that no longer agrees what "straight" means.

 Lesson 15 · Cosmology

Expanding Universe

Nobody is at the center, which is either humbling or exactly what you suspected about the universe. Watch space stretch itself thin and light redden with the effort.

The clocks remain, for the moment, silent.
