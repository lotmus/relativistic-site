The Light Clock

A clock can be made from two mirrors and one pulse of light. Watch the same clock from its own rest frame and from a platform as it moves past. The pulse follows a longer path in the platform frame; because light still travels at c, the platform measures more time between ticks.

For one complete tick, the clock itself measures Δτ = 2L/c. The platform measures Δt = γΔτ, where γ = 1/√(1−β²). At 0.60c, γ is 1.25: the platform measures 1.25 seconds for each second on the moving clock. The moving observer still sees the clock tick normally.
